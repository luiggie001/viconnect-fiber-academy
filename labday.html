Hands-On Lab Day

EN: Each certification includes one intensive hands-on lab day in Puerto Rico. Small groups ensure maximum practice time and direct feedback.
ES: Cada certificación incluye un día intensivo de laboratorio práctico en Puerto Rico. Grupos pequeños aseguran máximo tiempo de práctica y retroalimentación directa.

What You Will Practice

  • Fiber prep, cleaning & inspection.
  • Fusion splicing & mechanical splicing.
  • Connector installation & testing.
  • OTDR setup & basic trace reading.
  • Real troubleshooting scenarios.

What To Bring

  • Safety shoes (steel/composite recommended).
  • Safety glasses (we may provide extras).
  • Comfortable work clothing.
  • Notebook & pen.

All fiber tools, test equipment and consumables are provided during lab.

Location & Scheduling

EN: Lab sessions are held at designated training locations in Puerto Rico. Exact address and time are provided by email after enrollment.
ES: Las sesiones de laboratorio se realizan en lugares de entrenamiento designados en Puerto Rico. La dirección y hora exactas se envían por correo electrónico después de su matrícula.

To ask about lab availability, email: viconnectfiberacademy@gmail.com
