About Viconnect Fiber Academy

Viconnect Fiber Academy provides bilingual, industry-aligned fiber optic training designed for real job-ready results. Our hybrid training model combines online theory with a hands-on lab day delivered by a field technician with 20+ years of real-world experience in fiber installation, splicing, OTDR testing, and network troubleshooting.

We follow the best practices, standards, and training structure used by leading fiber organizations in the industry, preparing students for high-demand roles in ISP / OSP installation, fiber testing, FTTx deployment, and more.

Sobre Nosotros

Viconnect Fiber Academy ofrece entrenamiento bilingüe en fibra óptica, diseñado para preparar técnicos para el mundo laboral real. Nuestro modelo híbrido combina teoría en línea con un día de laboratorio práctico impartido por un técnico con más de 20 años de experiencia en instalación, empalme, pruebas OTDR y solución de problemas en redes de fibra óptica.

Nuestro programa sigue las mejores prácticas y estándares utilizados por organizaciones líderes en la industria, preparando a los estudiantes para roles de alta demanda en instalación ISP/OSP, pruebas de fibra, despliegue FTTx y más.

Instructor Experience

Your instructor brings 20+ years of real-world fiber optic experience including:

  • ISP/OSP fiber installation & troubleshooting
  • Fusion splicing for enterprise & carrier networks
  • OTDR testing, power budget calculations & diagnostics
  • FTTx deployment & modernization projects
  • Work on government, commercial & residential fiber systems

This ensures students learn directly from someone who has been in the field, solved real problems, and understands the technical and professional skills companies are hiring for today.
